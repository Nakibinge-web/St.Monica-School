Beyond the Classroom

At St. Monica Junior School Kasanje, we believe in nurturing the whole child. Our vibrant co-curricular programs offer students the opportunity to discover new passions, build teamwork, and develop leadership skills in a supportive, engaging environment.

Our Co-curricular Activities

Beyond the classroom, we offer a diverse range of activities that develop skills, foster teamwork, and create well-rounded individuals.

Ball Games

Pupils enjoy a range of games from football to netball. It teaches them about the importance of teamwork. Our pupils compete in a wide range of tournaments, giving them the chance to travel, meet, and play against children from elsewhere in their country.

Music & Performing Arts

Our music and performing arts program nurtures creative expression through singing, dancing, and drama. Students learn traditional Ugandan instruments and contemporary music, building confidence and stage presence through regular performances and concerts that celebrate our rich cultural heritage.

Student Clubs & Societies

Our diverse range of clubs and societies provides students with opportunities to explore their interests beyond academics. From the Scouting movement to the Red Cross Club, Environment Club, and Debate Society, students develop leadership skills, community awareness, and social responsibility while pursuing their passions.

Arts & Crafts

Our arts and crafts program encourages creativity and self-expression through various mediums including painting, drawing, pottery, and traditional Ugandan crafts. Students develop fine motor skills, artistic techniques, and cultural appreciation while creating beautiful works of art that showcase their imagination and talent.
