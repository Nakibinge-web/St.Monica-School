Curriculum Overview

St. Monica Junior School Kasanje follows the National Primary School Curriculum, enhanced with enrichment programs to foster well-rounded learners prepared for the challenges of the modern world.

school

National Curriculum

We adhere to the Uganda National Primary School Curriculum, ensuring all pupils receive a solid foundation in core academic areas.

stairs

Key Stages

The curriculum is structured into Lower Primary (P1-P3) and Upper Primary (P4-P7), with age-appropriate content and teaching methods for each stage.

Teaching Approach

Our innovative teaching methods ensure every student receives personalized attention and support to reach their full potential.

1

Child-Centered Learning

Lessons are interactive and tailored to meet the needs of each pupil, encouraging participation and curiosity.

2

Inquiry-Based Methods

Pupils are encouraged to ask questions, explore, and discover through hands-on activities and projects.

3

Continuous Assessment

Regular formative and summative assessments help track progress and inform teaching strategies.

4

Remedial & Enrichment Programs

Support is provided for pupils who need extra help, while advanced learners are challenged with enrichment activities.
