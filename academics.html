Academic Performance

The academic performance of St. Monica Junior School, Kasanje has consistently stood out in the region, with pupils excelling in both internal and national examinations. The school's commitment to quality teaching, combined with a supportive learning environment, has enabled learners to achieve high grades year after year. Teachers employ modern teaching methods that encourage critical thinking, creativity, and problem-solving, ensuring that students not only pass exams but also gain practical knowledge and skills for future success.

Beyond examination results, the school has built a reputation for nurturing well-rounded learners who excel in academics as well as co-curricular activities. Pupils regularly represent the school in district and national competitions, often emerging among the top performers. This culture of excellence is reinforced by dedicated staff, strong parental involvement, and a clear focus on discipline and hard work. As a result, the school continues to produce confident, capable, and academically outstanding graduates who are well-prepared for the next stage of their education.

School Policies

Clear guidelines that ensure a structured, safe, and productive learning environment for all students.

schedule

School Hours

Classes run from 7:30 AM to 4:00 PM, Monday through Friday. Students are expected to arrive by 7:15 AM for morning assembly. Early drop-off and after-school care programs are available for working parents.

info Gates close at 7:30 AM sharp
menu_book

Homework Policy

Homework is assigned to reinforce classroom learning and develop independent study habits. Age-appropriate assignments are given daily, with 30-45 minutes for lower primary and 1-2 hours for upper primary students.

check_circle Due dates must be respected
fact_check

Attendance Policy

Regular attendance is essential for academic success. Students must maintain at least 90% attendance. Absences must be reported by 8:00 AM and supported by a medical note or parent letter. Excessive absences may require a meeting with administration.

call Contact office for absences
gavel

Discipline Policy

We promote positive behavior through clear expectations and consistent consequences. Our approach focuses on respect, responsibility, and character development. Minor infractions receive warnings; serious or repeated issues involve parent communication and corrective action.
