Message from Our Director

Welcome to St. Monica Junior School! We are dedicated to providing a nurturing environment where every pupil can thrive. Our focus on holistic and Christian development ensures that each child reaches their full potential, both academically and personally.

Our commitment to excellence is reflected in our UNEB Center number, which guarantees that our pupils are well-prepared for national exams. We use innovative teaching methods to engage students, fostering a lifelong love of learning.

In addition to academics, we offer a range of co-curricular activities to enhance overall development. Our balanced approach supports pupils' growth in both their academic and personal lives, preparing them for future success.

Why Choose St Monica?

We are committed to providing a nurturing environment where each child can thrive academically, socially, and emotionally.
